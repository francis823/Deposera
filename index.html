How it works

Four steps. No arguments.

  1. Move-in photos

    We photograph every room before the keys are handed over.

  2. Held safely

    The deposit sits in a separate account all tenancy.

  3. Pre-inspection

    Before move-out, the tenant gets the damage list and can fix it.

  4. Money back

    Within 14 days, or 30 if repairs are needed.
