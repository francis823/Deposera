Our promise

Three things every deposit deserves

A deposit is often more than a month's rent, and for many tenants it's the largest sum they have saved. We treat it that way.

Held safely

Every deposit sits in a holding account, kept apart from our own money. Neither side can take it out alone.

Returned on time

Every step has a deadline, and both sides get reminders. Agreed money is paid within 3 working days.

Split fairly

Every deduction needs an amount, a reason and evidence. If you can't agree, an independent reviewer decides.

How it works

From move-in to money back

We hold the money and keep track of the deadlines. You each have a short list of things to do.

See every step

  1. The deposit comes to us

    The tenant pays Deposera before move-in, not the landlord. Both sides get a receipt.

  2. One shared record

    The landlord uploads a dated inventory with photos. The tenant adds notes in the first 7 days. Both of you see the same copy.

  3. A fair split at move-out

    The landlord proposes any deductions with evidence. The tenant accepts or challenges each item, and we pay out what's agreed.

For both sides

Neutral by design

Our fee stays the same whatever happens at move-out, so we have no reason to take a side.
