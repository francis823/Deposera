Why Deposera

No more deposits kept without a reason

A deposit is often more than a month's rent, and for many tenants it's the largest sum they have saved. Too often it ends in an argument. We change how it works.

Without Deposera

  • The landlord holds the tenant's money
  • Photos, if any, are taken by one side
  • Money is kept with no proof or invoice
  • The tenant finds out about damage after leaving
  • Weeks of messages to get the deposit back

With Deposera

  • Held safely in an account kept apart from our own money
  • Checked by us with neutral photos at move-in and move-out
  • Proof first: every deduction needs evidence and an invoice
  • Fix it first: the tenant sees the damage list before leaving
  • Returned on time within 14 days, or 30 if repairs are needed

How it works

From move-in to money back

We hold the money, do the inspections and keep track of the deadlines. You each have a short list of things to do.

See every step

  1. We inspect at move-in

    The tenant pays the deposit to us. We photograph every room before the keys are handed over, and both sides have 7 days to add anything.

  2. A pre-inspection before move-out

    We walk through the home with the landlord and list any damage. The tenant gets the list and can fix things themselves.

  3. Keys back, money back

    We check the home again at key return. Anything still not fixed is repaired by a company the landlord chooses, and only that cost comes out of the deposit.

Independent

On no one's side. Except the facts.

Tenants pay nothing. The landlord's letting agency, or the landlord, pays our fee, but it's the same whatever happens at move-out. We take the photos ourselves and every deduction needs proof, so nobody can keep a deposit without a real reason.
