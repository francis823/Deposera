FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers. Pick a topic or open a question.

Money and deadlines

How long until I get my deposit back?

Within 14 days of handing back the keys. If something needs repairing, within 30 days, minus the repair cost.

Why 14 and 30 days?

They follow Dutch rules: 14 days after the tenancy ends, or 30 days when damage costs are deducted.

How much does Deposera cost?

Nothing for tenants. The landlord's letting agency pays a one-off fee of 5% of the deposit, or the landlord does when renting without an agency. See pricing.

Can the landlord pass the fee on to me?

No. Dutch law stops landlords and their letting agents from charging tenants extra fees, and our fee is never taken from the deposit.

Can my landlord take money without telling me?

No. Money only comes out for damage we've checked, backed by an invoice, or when an independent reviewer decides it should.

Inspections and damage

Who takes the photos?

We do, at move-in and move-out. Both sides have 7 days after the keys are handed over to add their own notes and photos.

Can I fix damage myself?

Yes. After the pre-inspection you get the damage list. Anything you repair properly before key return isn't deducted.

Who chooses the repair company?

The landlord, for anything still not fixed at key return. The cost is backed by their invoice.

What if the landlord finds damage after I've left?

They have 14 days after key return to report it. We check it against our photos before anything is deducted.

What if I disagree with a deduction?

Tell us why. We review the evidence, and if you still disagree, an independent reviewer decides. See disputes.

About Deposera

If the landlord's side pays, are you on their side?

No. Our fee is the same whatever happens at move-out, we take the photos ourselves, and an independent reviewer has the final say.

When will the app be ready?

It's being built. You can already try the app prototype to see how it will work.

Is Deposera a real company?

No. It's a fictional Dutch company made for a school project. It holds no money, so please don't send payments or personal details.
