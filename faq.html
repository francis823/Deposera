Money and deadlines

How long will it take to get my deposit back?

Within 14 days of handing back the keys if nothing needs repairing. If something does, within 30 days, minus the repair cost.

Why 14 and 30 days?

They follow Dutch rules for returning a deposit: 14 days after the tenancy ends, or 30 days when costs for damage are being deducted.

How much does Deposera cost?

Tenants pay a one-off fee of 5% of the deposit when the tenancy starts. It's paid separately, so the full deposit is held for you. Landlords and letting agencies pay nothing. See what's included.

Can my landlord take money out of the deposit without telling me?

No. Money only comes out for damage we have checked, backed by a repair invoice, or when an independent reviewer decides it should. You can check the status of your deposit at any time.

Inspections and damage

Who takes the photos?

We do, at move-in and at move-out, so the record is neutral. Tenants and landlords have 7 days after the keys are handed over to add their own notes and photos.

Can I fix damage myself instead of paying for it?

Yes. After the pre-inspection we send you a list of any damage. Anything you repair properly before handing back the keys won't be deducted.

Who chooses the repair company?

If something is still not fixed when the keys come back, the landlord chooses the company. The cost taken from the deposit is backed by their invoice.

What if the landlord finds damage after I've left?

The landlord has 14 days after key return to report it. We check it against our move-out photos before anything is deducted.

What if I disagree with a deduction?

Tell us which item and why. We review the evidence first, and if either side still disagrees, an independent reviewer decides. Read about damage and disputes.

About Deposera

Is Deposera a real company?

No. Deposera is a fictional company based in the Netherlands, created for a school project. It holds no money and offers no real service, so please don't send it any payment or personal details.
