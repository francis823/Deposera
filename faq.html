Money and deadlines

Can my landlord take money out of the deposit without telling me?

No. Money only leaves when you have both agreed, when a deadline has passed after reminders, or when a reviewer has made a decision. You can check the status of your deposit at any time.

How much does Deposera cost a tenant?

Nothing. The landlord pays a one-off fee of 3% of the deposit, and it never comes out of your money. See pricing for a worked example and a fee calculator.

How long will it take to get my deposit back?

If nobody disputes anything, the longest it takes is 10 days for the landlord to propose a split, 10 days for you to respond, and 3 working days for the payment. It's often faster, because either step can finish early.

What if the other person doesn't respond?

We send reminders before each deadline. If a landlord proposes nothing within 10 days, the full deposit goes back to the tenant. If a tenant doesn't respond within 10 days, the landlord's proposal stands.

What if I disagree with a deduction?

Challenge that item before your deadline. Anything you don't challenge is paid out, and the challenged amount stays on hold until you settle it or a reviewer decides. Read about the disputes process.
