How it works

A short list each. We handle the rest.

We hold the money, take the photos, run the inspections and keep track of the deadlines. Tenants and landlords only have a few things to do.

Tenants

If you are a tenant

You pay the deposit to us, so you never have to chase a landlord to find out where it is.

  1. Sign up and invite your landlord

    Choose Deposera, invite your landlord, then pay the deposit and our one-off fee before you move in. You get a receipt on the working day the money arrives.

  2. Check our move-in report

    We photograph every room before you get the keys. If you spot anything we missed, you have 7 days after receiving the keys to add your own notes and photos.

  3. Fix anything from the pre-inspection

    Before you move out, we inspect the home with your landlord and send you a list of any damage. You can repair it yourself, which is often cheaper.

  4. Hand back the keys and get your money

    We check the home one last time. If nothing needs repairing, your deposit comes back within 14 days. If something does, it comes back within 30 days, minus the repair cost.

Landlords

If you are a landlord

We work for the tenant, but we're fair to you: you get a professionally checked property, one clear record and payment for real damage.

  1. Accept your tenant's invitation

    Your tenant chooses Deposera and invites you. You agree that we hold the deposit. Our service costs you nothing.

  2. We inspect and photograph at move-in

    You get the same report as your tenant. You have 7 days after the keys are handed over to add your own notes and photos.

  3. Join the pre-inspection

    Before the tenant moves out, we walk through the home together and agree a list of any damage. We share it with the tenant so they can fix it.

  4. Choose who repairs what's left

    If anything is still not fixed when the keys come back, you choose the repair company. The cost is taken from the deposit, backed by their invoice.

  5. Report anything we missed

    You have 14 days after key return to report other damage. We check it against our photos before anything is taken from the deposit.

Timeline

From move-in to refund

The usual order of events, from the first payment to the final payout.

  1. Before move-in The tenant signs up, invites the landlord and pays the deposit to Deposera. Both sides get a receipt.
  2. Move-in inspection We photograph every room and write the move-in report before the keys are handed over.
  3. First 7 days The tenant and landlord can add their own notes and photos to the report.
  4. During the tenancy The deposit stays in the holding account. Neither side needs to do anything.
  5. Before move-out We do a pre-inspection with the landlord and send the tenant a list of any damage to fix.
  6. Key return We check the home and take the move-out photos. Anything still not fixed is repaired by a company the landlord chooses.
  7. Within 14 days The landlord can report anything we missed. If nothing needs repairing, the full deposit goes back to the tenant.
  8. Within 30 days If repairs were needed, the tenant gets the deposit back minus the repair costs. Read about damage and disputes.
