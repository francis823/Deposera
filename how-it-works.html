How it works

A short list each. We handle the rest.

Tenants and landlords each have a few things to do. We hold the money and keep track of the deadlines.

Tenants

If you are a tenant

You pay the deposit to us, so you never have to chase a landlord to find out where it is.

  1. Pay the deposit before you move in

    Use the payment link in your invitation. You get a receipt on the working day the money arrives.

  2. Check the inventory in your first 7 days

    Add photos and notes about anything already damaged or dirty. This record protects you at move-out.

  3. Reply when the landlord proposes a split

    You have 10 days after their proposal to accept it or challenge any part. We remind you before the deadline.

  4. Get your money

    We pay agreed amounts within 3 working days. Tenants pay Deposera nothing, so no fee comes off your refund.

Landlords

If you are a landlord

You get one record for each deposit, and you don't have to look after the tenant's money yourself.

  1. Set up the tenancy

    Enter the address, move-in date and deposit amount, then invite your tenant. It takes a few minutes.

  2. Upload your inventory

    Add a dated inventory with photos before move-in. Your tenant sees the same copy.

  3. Propose any deductions after move-out

    You have 10 days. List each deduction with its amount and your evidence, such as a repair invoice. If you propose nothing, we return the full deposit to the tenant.

  4. Receive any agreed amount

    We pay it to your account within 3 working days of agreement. If part is disputed, only that part stays on hold.

Timeline

From move-in to refund

The usual order of events when nothing is disputed.

  1. Before move-in The tenant pays the deposit to Deposera. Both sides get a receipt.
  2. First 7 days The tenant checks the landlord's inventory and adds any notes and photos.
  3. During the tenancy The deposit stays in the holding account. Neither side needs to do anything.
  4. Move-out day The tenant returns the keys. Either side can confirm the tenancy has ended.
  5. Next 10 days The landlord proposes a split, or tells us to return the full deposit.
  6. Following 10 days The tenant accepts the proposal or challenges it. A challenge starts our disputes process.
  7. Within 3 working days We pay out the agreed amounts and close the record.
