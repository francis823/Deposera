How it works

A short list each. We handle the rest.

We hold the money, take the photos and track every deadline.

Your part

What you do

If you're a tenant

  1. Pay the deposit

    Pay it to us before you move in. Our service is free for you.

  2. Check our photos

    Add your own notes within 7 days of getting the keys.

  3. Fix what's listed

    After the pre-inspection, repair anything on the list yourself.

  4. Hand back the keys

    Money back within 14 days, or 30 if repairs are needed.

If you're a landlord

  1. Set up the tenancy

    You or your letting agency add it and invite the tenant.

  2. Get our report

    Add your own notes within 7 days of the key handover.

  3. Join the pre-inspection

    Agree the damage list with us before move-out.

  4. Repairs and reports

    Choose who repairs what's left. Report missed damage within 14 days.
