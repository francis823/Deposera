About

We take the worry out of the deposit.

We exist for one awkward part of renting: who holds the deposit, and who decides what happens to it.

The problem

The problem we solve

A deposit is often more than a month's rent. For a tenant, it can be the largest sum they have saved, and they hand it to someone they may have met only once.

Landlords have a different worry. They have to look after someone else's money for a year or more, then justify every deduction when the tenancy ends.

At move-out, one person is holding the money and the other is asking for it. That can turn a small cleaning bill into weeks of tense messages.

Our mission

Held safely, returned on time, split fairly

We make sure every deposit is held safely, returned on time and split fairly, with clear reasons for any money that is kept.

How we stay neutral

Four rules we never bend

  • We keep deposits apart from our own money.
  • We take the photos ourselves, so the record belongs to no one side.
  • Our fee is the same whatever happens at move-out, so we gain nothing from deductions.
  • Reviewers have no contact with either side before they decide.

Our team

Four teams, one deposit

Each team looks after one part of a deposit's journey, from the first photo to the final payout.

Inspections Team
Photographs every home at move-in and move-out, and runs pre-inspections.
Tenant Support
Helps tenants understand their record, damage lists and deadlines.
Agency & Landlord Accounts
Helps agencies and landlords set up tenancies and follow repairs.
Disputes Team
Reviews damage claims and assigns independent reviewers.
