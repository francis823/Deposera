# Deposera booth plan

Goal: stop people walking past, give them a 1-minute experience that proves our point, and end every visit with "vote Deposera".

Our message in one line: **"A deposit should only be kept for real, proven damage."**

## Interactive ideas (pick 2 or 3)

### 1. Coin toss: "Get your deposit back" (the main attraction)
- A cardboard house on one side, a big pocket on the other: glue an old jeans pocket to a board, or use a basket.
- Visitors throw gold chocolate coins from a line on the floor. If it lands in the pocket, they keep the coin.
- Put a small sticker on each coin: "Your deposit, returned. Vote Deposera".
- It's the banner come to life, and the coin they take home reminds them to vote.
- You need: about 50 chocolate coins, cardboard, tape and stickers.

### 2. "Fair or not?": vote on real deductions
- Print 5 cards, each with a situation. Visitors drop a token in a "Fair" or "Not fair" jar, then flip the card for our answer.
- It gets people talking and shows exactly why Deposera exists.

| Situation | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Carpet lightly worn after 2 years | **Not fair.** Normal wear isn't damage. |
| Oven left greasy at move-out | **Fair**, but only the real cleaning cost, with an invoice. |
| A crack that's already in our move-in photos | **Not fair.** It was already there. |
| A broken blind that isn't in the move-in photos | **Fair**, but the tenant could have fixed it first. |
| "General cleaning: €300", with no invoice | **Not fair.** No proof, no deduction. |

### 3. Spot the damage: 30-second challenge
- Show a move-in photo and a move-out photo side by side, printed on A3 or on the tablet. Visitors find the differences in 30 seconds.
- Keep a leaderboard on a whiteboard. The best score of the day gets a prize.
- It shows why neutral photos taken by us matter.

### 4. Live app demo on a tablet or laptop
- Open the app prototype at **"Before move-out"**. Let visitors switch items between "I'll fix it" and "Landlord repairs" and watch the money they get back change.
- Also open the website home page so they can drag the coin into the pocket.
- Investors love seeing a working product.

### 5. Sticker poll board
- Ask: **"Have you, or someone you know, lost a deposit without a clear reason?"** Visitors answer Yes or No with a dot sticker.
- A full "Yes" column draws people in and gives you a talking point with investors.

### 6. Photo moment
- A frame or sign saying "I got my deposit back!" People take photos and share them, which brings more visitors.

## End every visit with the vote

- Say the same line every time: *"If you liked it, vote Deposera!"*
- Put a small sign at the edge of the table explaining how to vote.
- Everyone leaves with a gold coin as a reminder.

## 30-second pitch

> "Have you ever lost a deposit without a real reason? Deposera fixes that.
> We hold the deposit in a separate account, and we take the photos ourselves at move-in and move-out, so the record is neutral.
> Before move-out we do a pre-inspection, so tenants can fix things themselves first.
> The money comes back within 14 days, or 30 if repairs are needed, and every deduction needs proof.
> Tenants pay nothing. The landlord's letting agency pays a one-off 5% fee.
> Try our app with the QR code, and if you like it, vote Deposera!"

---

## Investor questions: short answers

- **How do you make money?** A one-off fee of 5% of the deposit per tenancy, paid by the letting agency, or by the landlord if they rent without one. On a €1,500 deposit, that's €75.
- **Why would agencies pay?** We do the inspections, photos and payouts for them, and there are fewer disputes on their desk.
- **If the landlord's side pays, aren't you biased?** No. Our fee is the same whatever happens at move-out, we take the photos ourselves, and an independent reviewer has the final say in disputes.
- **Why don't tenants pay?** Dutch rules stop landlords and their agents from charging tenants extra fees. It also keeps our service free for the people with the most to lose.
- **Why 14 and 30 days?** They match the Dutch rules for returning a deposit.
- **What's next?** The app. You can try the prototype right now.
