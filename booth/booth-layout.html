Booth layout for tomorrow

Planned for a space of about 3 m wide × 2 m deep with one table. Visitors arrive from the aisle at the bottom of the plan.

Floor plan Seen from above

What goes where

  1. 1Roll-up banner in the back-left corner, turned slightly toward the aisle. It's the first thing people see.
  2. 2Coin toss: cardboard house and jeans pocket on the floor, in front of the banner.
  3. 3Throw line: tape on the floor, about 1–1.5 m from the pocket.
  4. 4Table along the front-right edge, leaving the left side open to walk in.
  5. 5Tablet with the games on a stand, facing visitors, in the middle of the table.
  6. 6Bowl of gold coins next to the tablet, as prizes.
  7. 7"Scan & play" sign and handout cards at the left end, nearest the entrance.
  8. 8"Fair or not?" tent cards and 2 jars (Fair / Not fair) with tokens, at the right end.
  9. 9Leaderboard screen (laptop on a box) on the back wall, raised so it shows over people's heads.
  10. 10Sticker poll board in the back-right corner: "Lost a deposit without a reason?"
  11. 11Extension cord along the wall and under the table, taped down.
  12. 12Thank-you tent card ("Thanks for visiting Deposera") at the front-right corner of the table.
  13. GGreeter in the aisle with coins: "Want to win your deposit back?"
  14. HGame host between the coin toss and the tablet.
  15. PPitcher behind the table: app demo and investor questions.

Visitor path (green arrows): banner catches their eye → greeter invites them → coin toss → game on the tablet or their phone → name on the leaderboard → they leave with a coin and a card.

Morning setup (about 30 min)

1. BannerSet up the roll-up first, in the back-left corner. 2. TablePush it to the front-right. Cover it with a dark cloth if you have one. 3. PowerRun the extension cord and tape it down so nobody trips. 4. ScreensTablet on …/games, laptop on …/games/#leaderboard. 5. GamesCoin toss target, throw line, tent cards, jars, coins, sign and cards. 6. ResetEmpty the Google Sheet, open …/games/#reset, then test a QR code with a phone.

Space is smaller?

  • Put the coin toss on the table (a small cup as the pocket).
  • Put the poll board on the table, or tape it to the front.
  • Use the 50 × 100 cm banner on the wall instead of the roll-up.

Keep in mind

  • Keep the front of the table clear so people can step up.
  • Turn screens toward the aisle, not toward you.
  • Swap roles every 30–45 minutes.
  • Everyone leaves with a coin and a card.

  • Tablet + stand + charger
  • Laptop + charger
  • Box to raise the laptop
