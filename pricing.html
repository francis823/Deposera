Pricing

One fee. Paid once. Never by the tenant.

Landlords pay a one-off fee of 3% of the deposit. Tenants pay nothing, and the fee never comes out of the deposit.

What's included

Everything, for one fee

A separate holding account

The deposit is kept apart from our own money for the whole tenancy.

One shared record

Inventories, photos, invoices and messages, all visible to both sides.

Deadline reminders

We remind each side before every deadline, so nothing slips.

Fast payouts

Agreed amounts are paid to the right person within 3 working days.

Independent dispute review

A reviewer with no contact with either side decides, with written reasons.

Help for both sides

Our tenant support and landlord accounts teams help with records and deadlines.
