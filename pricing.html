Pricing

One small fee. Everything protected.

Tenants hire Deposera with a single fee when the tenancy starts. It's paid separately, so the full deposit is held for them. Landlords pay nothing.

What's included

Everything a deposit needs, in one service

From the first photo to the final payout, we do the work that usually causes arguments.

Move-in and move-out photos

We photograph every room ourselves, so the record is neutral. Both sides can add notes within 7 days of the keys being handed over.

A pre-inspection before move-out

We walk through the home with the landlord and list any damage, so the tenant knows exactly what to expect.

The chance to fix it first

Tenants can repair damage themselves before key return. Anything left is repaired by a company the landlord chooses.

A separate holding account

The deposit is kept apart from our own money for the whole tenancy. Neither side can take it out alone.

Money back on time

Within 14 days of key return when nothing needs repairing, or within 30 days when something does.

Fair decisions

We check every damage claim against our photos. If either side still disagrees, an independent reviewer decides.

Who gets what

Good for both sides of the tenancy

Tenants

Protection from day one

  • Money is only kept for real, proven damage
  • You see the damage list before you leave
  • Fix things yourself and keep more of your money

One-off fee of 5% of the deposit, paid by the tenant when the tenancy starts.

Landlords

Pay nothing

  • Professional inspections at both ends of the tenancy
  • Choose your own repair company for anything left unfixed
  • 14 days after key return to report anything we missed
