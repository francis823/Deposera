Disputes

When something's damaged, there's a clear way through.

Most damage is fixed before the tenant leaves. If not, only the checked repair cost is deducted.

Damage and repairs

From pre-inspection to payout

  1. Pre-inspection

    We list any damage with the landlord.

  2. Tenant fixes

    The tenant can repair anything on the list.

  3. Key return check

    Anything left goes to the landlord's repair company.

  4. Late reports

    The landlord has 14 days to report damage we missed.

  5. Payout

    14 days, or 30 if repairs were needed.

If you disagree

A fair decision, with reasons

Only the amount in question stays on hold. Everything else is paid on time.

  1. Tell us why

    Challenge any item or cost, with your evidence.

  2. We review

    We compare our photos, the damage list and the invoices.

  3. Final say

    Still disagree? An independent reviewer decides within 15 working days.
