Damage & disputes

When something's damaged, there's a clear way through.

Most problems are spotted and fixed before the tenant even leaves. When they aren't, only the cost of the repair comes out of the deposit, and only once it's been checked.

Damage and repairs

From pre-inspection to payout

Checking for damage and reviewing a dispute are part of the service. Nobody pays extra for them.

  1. We do a pre-inspection with the landlord

    Before move-out, we walk through the home together and compare it with our move-in photos. We list any damage beyond normal wear.

  2. The tenant gets the list

    We send the tenant every item, with photos. They can fix any of it themselves before handing back the keys.

  3. We check again at key return

    We take the move-out photos and check each item on the list. Anything repaired properly is crossed off.

  4. The landlord chooses who repairs the rest

    For anything still not fixed, the landlord chooses the repair company. The cost comes out of the deposit, backed by the invoice.

  5. The landlord can report anything we missed

    The landlord has 14 days after key return to report other damage. We check every report against our photos before deciding.

  6. We pay out

    Within 14 days of key return if nothing needs repairing, or within 30 days if repairs were needed.

If you disagree

A fair decision, with reasons

The deposit belongs to the tenant unless a deduction is shown to be fair. Only the amount in question stays on hold. Everything else is paid out on time.

  1. Tell us which item and why

    Either side can challenge a damage item or a repair cost, and add any evidence they have.

  2. We review the evidence

    We compare our move-in and move-out photos, the pre-inspection list and the invoices, then make a decision.

  3. An independent reviewer has the final say

    If either side still disagrees, a reviewer who has had no contact with either of you decides. They send a written decision with reasons within 15 working days.

Evidence

What we look at

Because we take the photos ourselves, most questions can be answered from the record straight away.

Useful evidence

  • Our move-in and move-out photos and reports
  • Notes and photos either side added in the first 7 days
  • The pre-inspection list
  • Invoices from the repair company
  • Messages between you about the problem

Normal wear from everyday living, such as lightly worn carpet, is not a fair reason for a deduction.
